### The Guiding Principle of Historical Materialism

Published in London in 1859, the Preface contains Marx's most concise and definitive formulation of historical materialism:

> "In the social production of their existence, men inevitably enter into definite relations, which are independent of their will, namely relations of production appropriate to a given stage in the development of their material forces of production. The totality of these relations of production constitutes the economic structure of society, the real foundation, on which arises a legal and political superstructure and to which correspond definite forms of social consciousness."

### Key Principles

1. **Epochs of Social Revolution**: At a certain stage of development, the material productive forces of society come into conflict with the existing relations of production. From forms of development of the productive forces these relations turn into their fetters. Then begins an era of social revolution.
2. **Social Being and Consciousness**: It is not the consciousness of men that determines their existence, but their social existence that determines their consciousness.
