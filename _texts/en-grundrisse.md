### Introduction to the Grundrisse

The *Grundrisse der Kritik der politischen Ökonomie* (Rohentwurf) was written by Karl Marx in London during the global economic crisis of 1857-1858. Long unpublished until the Marx-Engels-Lenin Institute in Moscow brought it to light in 1939-1941, it represents the vital dialectical bridge between the *Paris Manuscripts of 1844* and *Capital*.

### Pivotal Theses

- **Dialectical Method**: The renowned 1857 Introduction articulates Marx's materialist epistemology: rising from the abstract to the concrete in thought while understanding that the real concrete remains the historical precondition of thought.
- **The Fragment on Machines**: Marx anticipates the colossal development of fixed capital and automated machinery, showing that capital's drive to reduce necessary labour-time to an absolute minimum creates the material preconditions for human emancipation from coercive labour.
