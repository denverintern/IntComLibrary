### The Defense of Invariant Marxism

Presented clandestinely in Lyon in January 1926 against the Gramsci-Togliatti leadership aligned with the degenerating Moscow center, the *Lyon Theses* constitute one of the greatest programmatic documents in the history of the communist movement.

### Core Programmatic Theses

1. **Doctrinal Invariance**: The party is not a reflection of shifting conjunctural moods or parliamentary arithmetic; it is the conscious organ representing the invariant historical programme of the proletariat from 1848 to the dictatorship of the proletariat.
2. **Rejection of Frontism**: Sharp condemnation of the tactics of the "united political front", "workers' governments", and organizational fusion with centrist or social-democratic organizations.
3. **Organic Centralism**: The inner life of the communist party must not depend on bureaucratic terror, mechanical discipline, or disciplinary expulsions, but on the organic convergence of tactics and doctrine across generations.
