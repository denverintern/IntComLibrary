### The Defense of Marxism

Presented clandestinely in Lyon in January 1926 against the Gramsci-Togliatti leadership aligned with Moscow, the *Lyon Theses* constitute one of the greatest programmatic documents in the history of the communist movement.

### Key Theses

- Defense of the historical invariant line of Marxism against revisionist tactics (united fronts, workers' governments, fusion with social democracy).
- Rejection of bureaucratic centralism and ideological terror within the party in favor of organic centralism founded on doctrinal coherence.
