### The Active Side of Materialism

Written in Brussels in the spring of 1845, the *Theses on Feuerbach* represent the breakthrough from Left Hegelianism and mechanical materialism into historical, dialectical materialism grounded in revolutionary proletarian praxis.

### Selected Theses

* **Thesis I**: The chief defect of all hitherto existing materialism is that the thing, reality, sensuousness, is conceived only in the form of the *object of contemplation*, but not as *sensuous human activity, practice*, not subjectively.
* **Thesis XI**: The philosophers have only interpreted the world in various ways; the point is to change it.
