### Historical Context

Published in February 1922 in *Rassegna Comunista*, this essay delivered a rigorous critique of the emerging democratic illusions within the international communist movement.

### Core Arguments

- **Democracy as Bourgeois Ideology**: The democratic principle rests upon the bourgeois fiction that society is an aggregate of equal individual voters possessing atomized opinions outside of material class relations.
- **Organic Centralism**: The communist party cannot subordinate its programmatic invariance or revolutionary discipline to formal numerical voting procedures or shifting majorities. Organizational structure must be organically bound to the historical programmatic continuity of the class.
