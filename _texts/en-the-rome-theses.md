### Tactical Theses of the Communist Left

Presented by the Left leadership (Amadeo Bordiga, Umberto Terracini) at the Rome Congress of the PCd'I in March 1922, the *Rome Theses* established the principled framework governing communist action within bourgeois society.

### Crucial Formulations

1. **Relation of Party and Proletariat**: The party does not encompass the whole working class, but constitutes its conscious, organized vanguard. Tactical action must constantly preserve the party's programmatic clarity.
2. **Critique of the United Front**: The party must only engage in united fronts on the *trade-union and economic terrain* (action united front), never in political pacts, governmental coalitions, or shared electoral programs with social-democratic parties.
