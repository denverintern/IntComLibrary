### Scientific Foundation of Scientific Socialism

*Das Kapital: Kritik der politischen Ökonomie* (Volume I) was published in Hamburg in 1867. It represents the culmination of more than two decades of rigorous investigation into the anatomy of bourgeois society.

### Structure and Core Concepts

1. **The Commodity and the Fetishism of Commodities**: The dual nature of the commodity as use-value and exchange-value, rooted in the twofold character of labour (concrete and abstract).
2. **The General Formula for Capital**: The circuit of commodity circulation ($C-M-C$) transformed into the circuit of capital ($M-C-M'$), where money begets surplus money through the purchase and consumption of living labour-power.
3. **Production of Surplus Value**: Absolute surplus value (lengthening of the working day) and relative surplus value (revolutionizing productivity to cheapen the basket of wage goods).
4. **The General Law of Capitalist Accumulation**: The rising organic composition of capital ($c:v$), the constant reproduction of the industrial reserve army, and the polarization of extreme wealth and collective misery culminating in the expropriation of the expropriators.
