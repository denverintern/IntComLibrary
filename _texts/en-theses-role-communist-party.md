### Historical Document

Drafted during the high tide of the European revolutionary wave of 1920, these theses laid down the foundational conditions for communist parties worldwide, drawing the balance sheet of the betrayal of the Second International and the victory of the Russian October.

### Central Principles

- The Communist Party is the vanguard of the working class, uniting its most steadfast, class-conscious, and revolutionary elements.
- Without a centralized political organization armed with revolutionary Marxist theory, the proletariat cannot successfully seize power or establish its dictatorship.
